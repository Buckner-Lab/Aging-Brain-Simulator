# Buckner et al., Figure 8C: a model change by a fresh agent

Screenshots and values from the version 78 file as changed by a second fresh AI agent (1.8 hours): each person's smoking exposure is the
average of the smoking curve over the years they were 18 to 40, and the hazard of vascular risk is multiplied by 2 raised to that exposure
(×1.07 to ×1.35). Vascular risk by 60 by birth decade before (gray, version 78) and after (green); the 11 reality checks of version 78; the new
dated insight. The agent's output file is not part of this package, so there is no analysis to rerun (Methods, Fresh-agent tests).
